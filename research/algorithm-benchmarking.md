Reproduce (from `website/`):

```bash

```


## Disagreements investigated

1. **Banded DTW with unequal lengths.** Read `tslearn/metrics/_masks.py` (`_sakoe_chiba_mask_generic`): for
   sz1 > sz2 the band is `width = sz1 − sz2 + radius` around the unscaled diagonal; NeuroMove's band follows the
   slope-scaled diagonal i·m/n ± r. Both are used in the literature; neither is "wrong". With equal lengths the two
   definitions coincide and the results are identical. MCSE only applies DTW to equal-length (101-sample) curves,
   so the difference never affects an MCSE result. Documented in `align.ts`.
2. **GMM K = 4 local optimum.** EM is non-convex; with 8 seeded restarts the NeuroMove fit for K = 4 is 1.85
   log-likelihood units below scikit-learn's. The BIC-selected K is unaffected here because K = 4 is far from the
   minimum. In strategy discovery the consequence of a worse optimum is conservative (a higher BIC for larger K
   favours fewer strategies). Not hidden: listed as a known limitation; increasing restarts would change results
   and therefore the component version.

## What is not benchmarked against a reference

- Component-count selection, the strategy refusal rules, PSDA and the longitudinal metrics are NeuroMove workflows
  composed of the benchmarked primitives; they are validated against known ground truth instead
  (`research/mcse-validation.md`).
- The functional permutation test was not compared with spm1d (GPL-3.0, not installed). Its family-wise error
  control is checked empirically in V7b (0/20 false dissociations at n ≈ 12 and 0/20 at n ≈ 24).
